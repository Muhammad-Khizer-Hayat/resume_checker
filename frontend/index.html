How each score is calculated

No hidden formula — here's exactly what feeds into your report, and how the model weighs it.

The model reads the job description for named skills, tools, and technologies, then checks your resume for evidence of each one — not just the word appearing, but real context around it.

Seniority, years, and the shape of your past roles are weighed against what the listing actually asks for — a senior-sounding JD scores differently than a junior one, even with identical skills.

Beyond skills — location, certifications, domain knowledge, and any other explicit "must-have" the job description lists get checked off (or flagged as missing) individually.

Every keyword shown is grounded in the actual text of your resume or the job description — nothing is invented. Matches are ranked by importance to the role; gaps are the ones worth adding first.
